🏨 Where to Stay

Whether you're looking for a luxury beachfront hotel, a budget-friendly resort or somewhere close to the wedding venue, here are three accommodation options to consider ✨.

  • 📍 Playa Blanca: Closest to the venue, with beaches, a scenic promenade and a relaxed atmosphere.
  • 🎉 Puerto del Carmen: Lively, with plenty of restaurants, bars and nightlife.
  • 🌊 Costa Teguise: A quieter resort with beaches and family-friendly options.

We recommend staying at the wedding venue the night before and the night of the wedding if possible. If you're staying elsewhere, please remember to organise transport to and from the venue.

🌋 A Little About Lanzarote

Lanzarote is known for its dramatic volcanic landscapes, beautiful beaches and distinctive architecture.

🌋 The island's volcanic landscape was dramatically reshaped by eruptions between 1730 and 1736, particularly around Timanfaya.

🎨 Local artist César Manrique helped shape Lanzarote's approach to architecture and tourism, encouraging development that respects the island's natural surroundings.

🍷 Lanzarote is also known for its distinctive vineyards, where vines grow in volcanic soil protected by curved stone walls.
